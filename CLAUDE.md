# Capra Lab: notes for working in this repo

JC's game hub at capra-lab.netlify.app; Netlify deploys `main` automatically. README.md has the layout, the profile system and how to make a game native. The rules that are easy to miss:

- **Status bar buffer on every screen.** Nothing may sit under the iPhone status bar: the page runs under it from the home screen and iOS blurs it. `viewport-fit=cover`, then every screen, top bar, HUD and fixed layer starts at `env(safe-area-inset-top)` plus a margin (bottom and landscape sides too). Check with `Emulation.setSafeAreaInsetsOverride` (see README).
- **Fill to the bottom of the screen** (JC, Nov 12 2026: "the gap that shows up at the bottom of the screen"). Since iOS 26 the home-screen app can get a page one status bar shorter than the screen (100%, 100dvh and clientHeight stop 62pt short), leaving a black strip at the bottom. Every page loads `<script src="/lab/fill.js"></script>` right after its viewport tag; on an iPhone that comes up short it sets `--lab-h` (the real screen height) and `--lab-gap` and stretches html and body. So a full-screen fixed layer is `inset: 0 0 auto; height: var(--lab-h, 100%)` (not `inset: 0`), a full-height screen is `var(--lab-h, 100dvh)`, and a button pinned to the bottom takes `- var(--lab-gap, 0px)` off its `bottom`. Test it by giving Playwright a 440×894 viewport with `screen.height` 956, `navigator.standalone` true and a 62px top inset.
- **Sideways uses the whole screen** (JC, Nov 15 2026: "All games need to maximize viewport in landscape mode. This is important for IRL especially, so I can mirror to my tv and play games for family game night"). Every page lays itself out for sideways, the lab's own pages with an `@media (orientation: landscape) and (min-width: 600px)` block (Mind Heist, Primal Survival, Swerve Planet and Don't Look! were already wide or built sideways): no narrow phone column in the middle (columns go to about 1180px), menus and results in two columns (`.col` wrappers that are `display: contents` upright), a play screen laid out sideways (the board or picture as big as the screen is tall with its controls beside it, a quiz's choices in one row). Upright stays exactly as it was. Check at 852×393 with insets `{ top: 0, bottom: 21, left: 59, right: 59 }`, and that nothing scrolls sideways.
- **The Capra Lab button on every game's main menu** (JC: the lab logo, left of the player's name, in the same place in every game). It's `<capra-lab-chip>` from `public/lab/chip.js` (`noname` when the game shows the name itself right after it, as Mind Heist does). It goes first in the main menu's top row so the logo lands `max(16px, safe-left)` in and `safe-top + 10px` down in every game; keep that corner clear. Glacial Maw loads it from the lab only under `/glacial-maw/`.
- **Capra's Lab** (JC's private side: `lab/crew.js`, `public/lab/capras-lab.js`). JJ-Capra is the owner with master controls; his crew (DA_ROMAN_EMPIRE first; the owner adds or removes others on the hub, and only names that already have a profile) gets the same early access. Members see a CAPRA'S LAB panel on the hub: games in testing, every Mind Heist deck with a Hidden/Public switch (owner only), the crew list (owner only). Every request proves the profile with its key; never put anyone's key in code or chat.
- **"JJ Brew" means hidden from the public.** When JC calls a deck or a game a JJ Brew, it starts hidden: a Mind Heist deck gets `"brew": true` in `deck_plan.json` (no date); a game gets `<script src="/lab/brew.js"></script>` first in its page's `<head>` and its hub tile goes in `#clGames` (Capra's Lab's "In testing") instead of the public grid. It goes public only when JC says so (take both out, or flip the deck's switch).
- **One results text for every game** (JC, Nov 11 2026: "I want this results text share consistent for all Capra lab games"). A game's results screen shows the group-chat card from `public/lab/share.js`: `<lab-text-share>` with a text built by `CapraShare.text({ emoji, game, no, sub, lines, grid })`: "<emoji> <Game>[ No. N][ · what was played]", the score lines (👑 the winner), then emoji rows (🟩 right, 🟥 wrong, 🟨 part way, ⬛ nothing, ⚡ fastest); the card adds the link and the Text it / Share / Copy buttons. Mind Heist has the same card in Preact.
- **The daily Card Draw** (JC, Nov 11–12 2026: out of Mind Heist, "for all Capra lab games... four cards. Some do nothing, others give you extra lives"). Four cards a day on the hub (`public/lab/card-draw.js`), flipped one by one; prizes wait in the lab player's hand (`lab/cards.js`, store `capra-lab-cards`, routes `/api/cards/*`). The cards and odds are `public/lab/cards-catalog.mjs`. A game offers its card with `<lab-card-offer k="...">` (`public/lab/card-offer.js`); cards marked `self` are spent by the page (`/api/cards/use`), the rest by the game's own server (`cards.take`, given back if the play is refused). Now: Mind Heist Mole and Hidden 50/50, Swerve Planet and Screensaver Extra Life, No Cap Cap Shield, Par-Tee Time Mulligan.
- **The lab wallet** (JC, Nov 12 2026: "diamonds amber and shards in Capra Lab. These are no longer just glacial maw unique"). One wallet per lab player (`lab/wallet.js`, store `capra-lab-wallet`, routes `/api/wallet/*`; what pays what is `public/lab/wallet-catalog.mjs`). JC's picks: pay by how rare (amber for finishing a game, shards for wins and bests, diamonds for rare feats a game's server checked), Glacial Maw linked now (its amber, Rift Shards and diamond gems are the wallet), spent in the avatar shop. A game page claims its pay with `CapraWallet.earn(game, event, ref)` from `/lab/wallet.js` at the end of a game (capped per game a day, a ref pays once); a game with a server pays with `wallet.pay()` (Goat Getaway, Screensaver, Masters, Mind Heist's old diamonds). The Capra Lab button shows the balances under the name.
- **Avatars** (JC, Nov 13 2026: a comic character, "person or animal", basics free, extras cost by rarity, 2D now and a hologram later). The catalog and prices are `public/lab/avatar-catalog.mjs` (common items cost amber, rare shards, legendary diamonds); `drawAvatar(look)` and `lookFor(handle, saved)` from `public/lab/avatar.mjs` draw any player (no saved look yet: a starter seeded from the handle). The wallet keeps `items` and `look` (`/api/wallet/buy`, `/dress`, `GET /looks?h=`). The Avatar Studio is `/avatar/`; the Capra Lab button shows the avatar and opens it. New art goes in named `data-layer` groups so the hologram can use it; a new item needs its drawing in `avatar.mjs` and its entry in the catalog (the test checks every item draws). The drawing must stay valid XML (no attribute twice on a tag: `drawAvatar` runs `oneEach` and the test checks it), because as a picture (the selfie, an `<img>`) Safari draws nothing at all for an SVG with one slip.
- **Invites reach the Home Screen app by hand-off** (JC, Nov 13 2026: a text invite "goes to my browser. Can it... push it to that?"). iOS opens every link from Messages in Safari and no web page can open a Home Screen app, so `/lab/fill.js` keeps the tapped link (`window.__labLanding`, before a game takes the code off the address) and loads `/lab/handoff.js` on iPhones and iPads: Safari leaves the invite at `/api/handoff/drop` for 5 minutes ("Got the app?" card) and the Home Screen app claims it each time it opens or comes to the front ("Your invite is here" → Show invite). `lab/handoff.js` matches by profile, else by the phone (network or city plus iOS version, screen, time zone, language), and opens it once. A new invite link needs nothing extra as long as its game page loads fill.js and reads the code from the address it opens with.
- **The golf handicap** (JC, Nov 13 2026: "a handicap for players to select in their profile"; his picks: strokes off the score, net decides Masters, changed at most once a week). It lives on the lab profile (`hcp`, `hcpAt` in `lab/profiles.js`, `POST /api/profile/handicap`, public in `GET /api/profile`); the numbers are `public/lab/handicap-rules.mjs` (0 to 18 strokes over 18 holes, a shorter round gets its share, rounded). `<lab-handicap>` from `/lab/handicap.js` shows and changes it (hub profile screen, Par-Tee Time's setup, online lobby and Masters cabinet). Masters gets it through `verify` and a try keeps the handicap it started with; in a friend match a handicap anywhere means the net decides (player two on your phone picks one in setup, online each phone sends its own, the hustler plays scratch).
- **Trophy rooms and trap houses** (JC, Nov 14 2026: anyone can watch a room "almost like they are given security camera access but they can only get to the room after making it through the trap house"; his picks: all four kinds of trap, the raider's pick of one trophy or the loot, caught means locked out of that room for the day and the owner gets amber, trophies are Masters plus every rare feat that pays a diamond). `/trophy-room/` (`public/trophy-room/`), server `lab/heist.js` at `/api/heist/*` (store `capra-lab-heist`), rules `public/lab/heist-rules.mjs`, the questions and holes `lab/heist-library.mjs` (made by `scripts/make-heist-library.mjs`: rerun it after adding Mind Heist decks or Par-Tee Time holes). Every `wallet.pay()` with diamonds mints a trophy (pass `trophy: false` for anything that isn't a feat); each kind has its art in `public/trophy-room/art.js` (`trophyKind` in heist-rules). The vault is a treasure trove (JC, Nov 14 2026: "a treasure trove of relics trophies and coins": stone, torches, a coin heap that grows with the trophies and loot, relics, chests; the selfie draws the same trove), and the room's trophy list is THE TROVE. Four traps a house to start (lab levels add more), any kind in any slot; one try a room a day. The golf and goat traps play in their games via `#trap=<raid>` and open once (leaving part way trips the alarm), so keep those modes working when changing Par-Tee Time's solo round or Goat Getaway's pen. The selfie is drawn on a canvas (`selfie.js`); its words are canvas text because SVG pictures can't use the page's fonts.
- **Daily boards** (JC, Nov 14 2026: "Three clue crew, and the puzzle, mind heist, and no cap to have a daily challenge leaderboard. More trophies to win. The most points the fastest wins"). Later that night: "Par-Tee Time needs a daily three hole challenge with a leaderboard and trophy too". Boards in `public/lab/daily-boards.mjs` (Three Clue Crew and Puzzled Spin a board per tier, Mind Heist's daily drop, No Cap a board per shelf, Par-Tee Time's Daily 3); server `lab/daily.js` at `/api/daily/*` (store `capra-lab-daily`). Most points, then fastest, then first in; only the first run of the day counts. At midnight Pacific (settled lazily) the #1 of a board with `WIN_MIN` (2) or more players gets `WIN_PAY` (💎 1) by `wallet.pay`, which mints the trophy. Par-Tee Time's Daily 3 is three holes a day dealt by `golfDeal` (one from each third of the courses, from `GOLF` in `lab/heist-library.mjs`: rerun the library script after adding holes), posted hole by hole (`CapraDaily.golf`; a hole left part way goes down as double par), fewest net strokes wins (`low` boards rank the other way), shown to par, its trophy a golf ball (`trophyKind` 'golf'). Games without a server use `CapraDaily.start/finish` from `/lab/daily.js` (the server checks the answers and times from the first start); Mind Heist and No Cap post from their servers (`daily` passed to their handlers; No Cap's daily is "Today's ten", `kind: 'daily'` rooms). Every game's menu has a 🏆 Today's board button (`CapraDaily.open`) and its results a `<lab-daily-board>`; the hub has TODAY'S BOARDS (`<lab-daily-today>`). A new daily game: add its boards, post its runs, add its trophy art.
- **One profile for every game.** Players are `localStorage['capra-lab.profile']` = `{ name, handle, code }` (names up to 17 characters, `NAME_MAX`, and every game shows all 17); servers check keys with `lab/profiles.js` (`verify`). Games keep their own stats keyed by handle. New players go to `/#profile` for the key-card walkthrough.
- **One place per route.** A path is mapped either in `netlify.toml` or by a function's `config.path`, never both.
- **Netlify treats `/x` and `/x/` as the same path.** Never write a redirect from `/x` to `/x/`: it loops and the page never loads (the local dev server doesn't show this). Link games with the trailing slash.

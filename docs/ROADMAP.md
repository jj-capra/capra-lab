# Capra Lab roadmap (JC, Nov 11 2026)

JC's words, kept whole so later work starts from them:

> Capra Lab is my families creation, but it's a lab for all. Let's bring Draw a Card out from Mind
> Heist and for all Capra lab games. Card Draw will be a daily task. I no longer want it unique to
> just Mind Heist. and it is four cards. Some do nothing, others give you extra lives in Swerve
> Planet, or screensaver, and other games, or a good amount of items with a similar advantage like
> hidden 50/50 is for Mind Heist.
>
> I also want diamonds amber and shards in Capra Lab. These are no longer just glacial maw unique.
> and this opens a way to accessorize and individualize their avatar that only shows as this profile
> pic and as an impressive 3D hologram in their trophy room. That's right, I want a trophy room and
> it is on top of a trap room of different obstacles. Each user created their trap house from a
> library of hard trivia questions, tough golf holes, an impossible goat getaway, etc. They start
> with a max of four traps but they can all be the same thing or mix matched. When they level up in
> the lab form entry level scientists to a beautiful mind level of expertise. Then their trap house
> gets more traps. Defending their trophy room is important because if another user can get to
> theirs they would lose a trophy or lute and take a selfie capturing their trophy room and behind
> is the other users hologram. This should be epic for trolling and for sharing on social media or
> in text message.
>
> But first, in Par-Tee Time I want double the courses. Then allow users to create a mixed 18 holes
> of two different courses they can save and send to a anyone in text as a challenge. This is
> Masters Course with different difficulty levels of holes to create a unique set they can practice
> at and challenge friends at. If they maintain their course best for a whole week they get a
> trophy. Each person they beat per match they get lute. This challenge is not in real time. It is
> just a chance at three challeneges to best the course designer. This would mean the designer can
> come back and to best any new records that best them in the same amount of try's (three).

## The order

1. **Par-Tee Time: eight courses and the Masters Course** (live Nov 12 2026). Trophies and loot
   earned here are kept per player so the trophy room can show them later.
2. **Card Draw for the whole lab** (built Nov 12 2026): a daily draw of four cards on the hub (out of
   Mind Heist); some duds, some perks per game. JC's picks: flip all four every day; first games
   Mind Heist (Mole, Hidden 50/50), Swerve Planet and Screensaver (Extra Life), No Cap (Cap
   Shield), Par-Tee Time (Mulligan). Goat Getaway (a free hint) and the rest can follow.
3. **Diamonds, amber and shards in Capra Lab** (out of Glacial Maw): one wallet per profile.
4. **Avatars**: bought and dressed with the wallet; the profile picture everywhere, a 3D hologram in
   the trophy room.
5. **Lab levels**: entry-level scientist up to beautiful mind; levels unlock more traps.
6. **Trophy rooms and trap houses**: each player's trophy room sits on a trap house they build from
   a library (hard trivia, tough golf holes, an impossible Goat Getaway...), four traps to start.
   Get through someone's traps and you take a trophy or loot and a selfie in their trophy room with
   their hologram behind you, made for sharing by text.

      HOW TO PLAY

      • Look. A sign that came out wrong (a misprint, burnt-out lights or terrible design), or the setup of a joke.
      • Flip through. Four cards, A to D. Swipe or tap to flip through them before the fuse runs out.
      • Call it. The writing fizzles out. Tap the letter you remember.
      • Reveal. 100 for a right answer, up to 100 more for speed, and 50 more if you were the first in the room.
      • Today’s drop is the same ten cards for everyone, every day: Wrong Signs one day, jokes the next.
      • Host a party to play any deck with up to 12 friends, each on their own phone. Late Night decks are for high school and up.

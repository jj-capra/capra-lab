HOW TO PLAY

  • Drag anything along its length to clear a path, then get the goat out the red gate. Nothing turns or jumps.
  • One slide, any distance, is one move. Par is the fewest moves it can be done in.
  • Today’s pen is the same for everyone: a Kid’s pen on Monday, up to the G.O.A.T. on Sunday.
  • The clock starts when you open the pen. Your first getaway is your time.
  • Get out: +1 💎. Do it in par: +1 more, even on another try. Diamonds go to Mind Heist, for its daily card draw.
  • The fastest getaway of the day wins a Blue Ribbon at midnight (Pacific time).
  • Stuck? 💡 shows the best next move from wherever you are. On today’s pen each hint adds 15 seconds to your time, and a day with hints doesn’t earn the par diamond. Hints are free in practice.
  • Practice pens never run out, and nothing is timed against anyone.

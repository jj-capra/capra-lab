HOW TO PLAY

  1. Every picture is a comic art piece cut into square tiles. Some tiles are turned the wrong way.
  2. Tap a tile to spin it clockwise. Swipe left on it to spin it back.
  3. Keep spinning until the whole picture looks right. Every piece is mirrored, so the left side matches the right and the top matches the bottom.
  4. K–1 and 2nd–3rd: the finished picture shows in the corner, and tiles get a ✓ when they're right. Middle and high school: hold PEEK to see it (costs 3 seconds).
  5. Five pictures a run, from two-tone shapes up to a masterpiece. Fast pictures build a Spin Streak. Today's Spin is the same five for everyone at your level.

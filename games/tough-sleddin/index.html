HOW TO SLED

  1. Your goat zooms down the hill on its saucer. It goes faster and faster.
  2. Hold the ◀ and ▶ arrows at the bottom to steer (or the arrow keys on a keyboard).
  3. Dodge the trees, rocks, snowmen and ice. Hit a snow ramp to fly over logs, fences and crevasses.
  4. On the glacier, shiny ice is slippery: you slide sideways!
  5. One crash and that world is over. Three worlds, then your final score is all three added up.

NO 3D HERE

Tough Sleddin needs 3D graphics (WebGL), and this browser didn't give them. Try Safari or Chrome, or turn off any setting that blocks WebGL.

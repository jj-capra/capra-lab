    HOW TO PLAY

    1. Pick a level and a deck. Turn the phone sideways.
    2. The guesser holds the phone on their forehead, screen facing everyone else. Don't look!
    3. Everyone else gives clues: describe it, act it out, make the sound. Just don't say the word.
    4. Got it? Flip the phone UP (screen to the ceiling). Stuck? Flip it DOWN to pass.
    5. Get as many as you can before time runs out, then pass the phone to the next guesser.

    No tilt on this phone? GOT IT and PASS buttons show up on the screen instead, so a friend can tap.

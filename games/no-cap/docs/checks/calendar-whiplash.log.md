## Changes
- cw01: the cap "hosted the Macy's parade for 20 years" was the only claim with a round number. Changed to "co-hosted ... for 19 years". It is still plainly false: Mental Floss gives Macy's 1962 to 1971 (ten years), and the Television Academy confirms her 20 years were with the Rose Parade. Real: "about ten" changed to "ten". Guinness confirmed 79 years (debut 1939, as of Feb 2018) and her birth on Jan 17, 1922. Sliced bread was first sold July 7, 1928 (Wikipedia).
- cw02: "was invented while Beethoven was still alive" failed one reading: Wikipedia's Lighter article says the first lighters were converted flintlocks, described in 1662, before Beethoven was born. Changed to "[had a hydrogen-burning model invented while Beethoven was still alive]". It now holds for Döbereiner's lamp (1823) and its model, Fürstenberger's hydrogen lighter (1780). Beethoven died Mar 26, 1827 (Britannica), and that label is gone. "Before the friction match" holds under every reading (1662, 1780 or 1823 against Walker's 1826).
- cw03: no change. Confirmed: born May 24, 1819; she proposed; died Jan 22, 1901 (Royal Mint). Napoleon died May 5, 1821; the Wrights flew Dec 17, 1903 (Wikipedia). BFI's Scenes at Balmoral was filmed Oct 3, 1896.
- cw04: no text change. Santa Fe was founded in 1607 and made capital in 1610 (Wikipedia), so it is before 1620 and 1616 either way. Shakespeare died Apr 23, 1616 (Wikipedia: 1616). New Mexico was the 47th state, and Sibley held the city in March 1862. Pilgrims 1620 left as textbook (label added).
- cw05: no text change. Bain's patent was May 27, 1843, and he was apprenticed to a clockmaker in Wick (Wikipedia). Le Gras dates to 1827. The pantelegraph was open to the public from Feb 1863, with official operation from Feb 16, 1865: during the Civil War either way. Bell 1876 left as textbook (label added).
- cw06: replaced the mirror source loki.editorial.aetnd.com with Mental Floss (White House phone 1877). The palace was finished in 1882 (KHON), so the cap is plainly false. Electric lights came four years before the White House (ETHW), and it was the government building until 1969 (KHON). Eiffel 1889 left as textbook (label added).
- cw07: no text change. Opened May 24, 1883; 21 elephants May 17, 1884; officially named in 1915 (NYPL). The Statue was dedicated Oct 28, 1886 (NWE), and the monument's capstone set Dec 6, 1884 (NPS).
- cw08: no text change. Born Sept 6, 1874; Coolidge at Black River Academy; check dated Jan 31, 1940; died Jan 1975 (Wikipedia). Moon walk July 20, 1969 (Guinness). Grant's term left as textbook (label added).

The honorary degree came on May 2. Born May 4, 1852, died Nov 16, 1934, 10 years old in July 1862 (Wikipedia). Lincoln 1861 and Mickey 1928 left as textbook (labels added).

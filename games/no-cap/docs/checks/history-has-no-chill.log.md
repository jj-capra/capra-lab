## Changes
- hn01: "Haiti became the first country born from a successful slave revolt" → "[Haiti became a nation after a successful revolt by enslaved people]". Wikipedia hedges ("one of the only known slave rebellions... that led to the founding of a state"), so the "first" wording came out. The metric system is confirmed by Wikipedia (devised in the early Revolution, launched in France in 1799).
- hn02: the earmuffs claim was wrong as written. Smithsonian: Greenwood had the idea at 15, but "It wasn't the first earmuff. It was an improved earmuff." → "[a 15-year-old in Maine came up with an improved design for earmuffs]".
- hn05: the Mansa Musa claim (gold's value dropped for years) is disputed. Wikipedia's Mansa Musa article cites the historian Warren Schultz, who says the drop was "well within normal fluctuations". → Replaced with "[a European world map from 1375 shows Mali's king Mansa Musa]" (Catalan Atlas, 1375). Real no longer gives the 27 BC date, so every detail in it is sourced (Julius Caesar article: dictator, never emperor; Augustus first emperor).
- hn07: Wikipedia calls Annie Edson Taylor "the first person to survive" a barrel trip over Niagara → claim now "the first person to survive going over Niagara Falls in a barrel was a 63-year-old teacher". Walter Arnold is confirmed by Guinness World Records (28 Jan 1896, Paddock Wood, Kent, 8 mph in a 2 mph zone). Real reworded to "in Kent, England".
- hn08: "Lady Jane Grey ruled England for just nine days" is arguable (Wikipedia: 9 days from her proclamation, about 13 from Edward VI's death) → "[Lady Jane Grey was deposed nine days after being proclaimed queen]". Real said Catherine moved to Russia "at 14", but Wikipedia says 15 → "as a teenager".
- hn09: "Super Glue was discovered by people trying to make clear gun sights" is not supported. Wikipedia's Harry Coover article says it was found in 1942 while looking for cockpit-cover materials. → "[Super Glue was discovered in World War II and rejected for being too sticky]". Bubble wrap: the inventors "originally tried to sell it as wallpaper" → "[the inventors of bubble wrap first tried to sell it as wallpaper]". The cap was lengthened so it isn't the shortest claim ("Velcro was invented by an engineer who studied how gecko feet stick"). Real now says burrs on "his clothes and his dog" (George de Mestral, 1941).
- hn12: "the first recorded labor strike" is hedged in the source ("may have been the first sit-down strike in recorded history") → "[ancient Egyptian tomb builders went on strike when their supplies ran late]" (Deir el-Medina, c. 1170 BC). Real: Khufu "around 2600 BC, more than a thousand years before Tutankhamun" (the old "more than 1,200 years" was too tight a margin).

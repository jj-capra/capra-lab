# wait-when: fact-check log (kids)

Second check (Nov 9 2026), on the one-subject version of the deck. Every claim was checked against the cited page or a new one, and every comparison was redone with its arithmetic.

- ww01 Woolly mammoths: no change. Wikipedia: Wrangel Island mammoths lasted until 4,000 years ago, centuries after the Great Pyramid (about 2560 BC); longest known tusk 4.05 m (13.3 ft); closest living relative is the Asian elephant. First Olympics 776 BC, about 1,200 years after the last mammoths.
- ww02 T. rex: `real` said "roughly one million years" → LiveScience gives 67 to 66 million years ago but says the start could reach back to 68, so `real` now says one or two million years. Confirmed: Stegosaurus about 150 million years ago vs T. rex about 67 (Gizmodo, from Smithsonian), so the gap to Stegosaurus is at least 77 million years even at 145, against 66 to 68 million to us; Triceratops at the same time (LiveScience); 23.5-hour days 70 million years ago (AGU).
- ww03 Pluto: claim 0 (after Mickey's first cartoon, 1928) and the cap (before the Wright brothers, 1903) could not both be true, so anyone who knows Mickey came after airplanes had a 50/50 → new cap "was discovered before the first Winter Olympics were held" (Chamonix, Jan 1924, Britannica; Pluto 1930; even the second Games, 1928, came first). Mickey: Steamboat Willie Nov 18, 1928 (D23), 15 months before Pluto. Venetia Burney, 11, Oxford, and 1930 to Aug 2006 = 76 years (Wikipedia).
- ww04 The Statue of Liberty: "worked as a lighthouse for its first 16 years" is the disputed claim the last check removed (1886 to 1902 vs 1887 to 1901) → "stopped letting visitors climb up into its torch in 1916" (Islands, New World Encyclopedia: closed after the 1916 Black Tom blast and never reopened). Cap "...before it sailed to America" was the longest claim → "...before it sailed away". Confirmed: dedicated Oct 28, 1886 vs Eiffel Tower 1889 (official Tour Eiffel site); Coca-Cola May 8, 1886 (Coca-Cola Company); finished July 1884, arrived June 17, 1885, 214 crates (NWE).
- ww05 Sharks: "about 200 million years before the first dinosaurs" → "more than 150 million years". Sharks about 450 million years ago (Discover Wildlife, CMNH); oldest confirmed dinosaurs about 230 million, and a 2026 study puts their start at 240 to 250 million (Greek Reporter). That gives a gap of 200 to 220 million, or right at 200 under the new dates, while 150 holds under every estimate. Trees 385 million years ago (Discover Wildlife); cartilage and thousands of teeth (CMNH).
- ww06 Oreo cookies: "started out as a copy of an older cookie called Hydrox" judges Nabisco's intent (Wikipedia says "imitation", but that's arguable) → "came out four years after a look-alike cookie called Hydrox" (1908 vs March 6, 1912, Wikipedia). Alaska and Hawaii now web-checked: Alaska Jan 3, 1959, Hawaii later in 1959 (National Constitution Center), so NOT WEB-CHECKED is gone. Sliced bread July 7, 1928 (Wikipedia). Teddy bear: NPS dates the Berryman cartoon to Nov 1902 with Michtom's bear right after, so `real` now says "around 1902" and "about ten years later".
- ww07 Hot air balloons: "120 years before the first airplane flight" plus the cap "before the Declaration of Independence" contradicted each other (1903 minus 120 is 1783, after 1776) for anyone who knows both dates → "carried people before George Washington became president" (Nov 21, 1783 vs April 30, 1789, Wikipedia; 5.5 years). Sheep, duck and rooster on Sept 19, 1783, and the family paper factory (EBSCO).
- ww08 The Moon: no change. Magnavox Odyssey Sept 1972, the first home console (Wikipedia), vs Apollo 11 in 1969; Apollo 17 Dec 1972 (Columbia) vs Star Wars May 25, 1977 (Wikipedia), 4.4 years; far side gets as much sunlight (NASA StarChild); Shepard's two balls, Feb 6, 1971 (USGA).
- ww09 Nintendo: cap confirmed plainly false. Nintendo still makes hanafuda cards, including Mario sets (Kotaku, 2022; Wikipedia Hanafuda: "continues to produce cards in Japan"). `src` moved to Kotaku; `real` "card games" → "still makes them, even Mario and Pokémon decks". Founded Sept 23, 1889 (Wikipedia) vs basketball Dec 21, 1891 (Mass Moments) and the Ferris wheel June 21, 1893 (Smithsonian); Daiya taxi in the 1960s (Nintendo Life).
- ww10 Barbie: Gagarin was cited to a page that doesn't mention him → Sky at Night (April 12, 1961); Barbie March 9, 1959 at 3 dollars (WFLA), two years earlier. Miss Astronaut 1965 (NASM); Barbara Millicent Roberts (History UK).
- ww11 LEGO: no change. Fox9: founded 1932 by a carpenter, plastic bricks 1949, "leg godt"; Disneyland July 17, 1955 (Wikipedia).
- ww12 The Great Wall of China: no change. Wikipedia: first walls 7th century BC (Roman Empire 27 BC), Ming watchtowers 1567 to 1570, sticky rice mortar, can't be seen from the Moon with the naked eye.

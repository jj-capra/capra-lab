# No Cap subjects (one card each, in the whole game)

Every card is about one subject, and **no subject appears twice anywhere**: not on two cards, two
decks or two shelves. Before you write a card, check this list, then add your subject on its own
line at the end with your shelf in front: `echo "- [kids] Abraham Lincoln (deck-id)" >> games/no-cap/docs/subjects.md`
(append only; four writers share this file).

## Lanes (so the four shelves don't reach for the same subjects)

- **Kids**: presidents George Washington, Abraham Lincoln, Theodore Roosevelt; animals and
  dinosaurs; space (planets, the Moon); toys and games (LEGO, Nintendo, Pokémon); foods and candy;
  landmarks kids know (the Statue of Liberty, the Great Wall); kids' movies and books.
- **Middle School**: presidents Thomas Jefferson, John Quincy Adams, William Howard Taft, Herbert
  Hoover; explorers; ancient Egypt, Greece and Rome (Cleopatra, the Colosseum); inventors (the
  Wright brothers, Thomas Edison); the space race; video games and YouTube; sports and the Olympics.
- **High School**: presidents John Adams, James Madison, Ulysses S. Grant, Franklin D. Roosevelt,
  Calvin Coolidge; scientists (Einstein, Marie Curie, Isaac Newton); revolutions and empires
  (Napoleon, the Ottoman Empire); composers and bands; tech companies (Apple, Google, Amazon);
  famous universities; athletes.
- **Young Adults**: presidents Martin Van Buren, James Garfield, Chester A. Arthur, Grover
  Cleveland, Rutherford B. Hayes, Dwight D. Eisenhower; strange historical figures; world cities
  and buildings; the internet and memes; streaming, film and music after 2000; money, companies
  and work; social media platforms (not their living owners).

No living politicians, no current partisan figures. Living public figures only for harmless,
well-documented facts.

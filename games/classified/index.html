        HOW IT WORKS

        • Briefing. The host opens a file and everyone joins with the code. Build your agent and get a codename.
        • Missions. You get three secret missions when the night starts and a new one every 45 minutes. Pull them off without anyone noticing, then mark them done. You can swap two you hate.
        • Catch a spy. Think someone’s working a mission on you? Accuse them and pick their mission out of four. Right: it’s burned and you score. Wrong: they score. Three tries a night.
        • Who Said It. New questions drop through the night. Answer them all; nobody sees your answers until the reveal.
        • Yearbook. Vote for an agent on every award. No voting for yourself.
        • Declassified. When the host is ready, the file opens on every phone: guess who wrote each answer, see every mission (done, burned or never pulled off), hand out the awards and crown the top agent. Then share the highlights.

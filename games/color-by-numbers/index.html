HOW TO COLOR

  1. Pick your level, then pick any picture.
  2. Tap a color at the bottom. Every spot with its number lights up gray.
  3. Tap the spots to fill them in. Hold your finger down and slide to fill lots of spots at once!
  4. Can't find one? Tap 🔍 and it shows you where.
  5. Paintbrush and Stained Glass have lots of tiny pieces: pinch the picture to zoom in, and drag to move around.
  6. Fill every spot to finish your masterpiece, then save it or text it to the family. Your coloring is saved, so you can come back any time.

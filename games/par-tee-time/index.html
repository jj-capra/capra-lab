        HOW IT WORKS

        • Pick any 8 holes from any course, or go by theme: all eight highways, all eight clocks…
        • You get one chance to set the course best. Whatever you score is posted.
        • Send the invite. Anyone signed in gets three tries to beat it. Lower total wins; a tie doesn’t.
        • Take the best: 💰 1 loot, and whoever you took it from gets three fresh tries to take it back.
        • Someone uses all three and misses: the holder gets 💰 1 loot.
        • Hold the best seven days straight: 🏆 a trophy for that course.
        • Handicaps count: the net score (your score less your handicap’s strokes) takes the course best.
        • Practice is free and never counts. Quitting a try burns it.

HOW IRL WORKS

  • Pick a challenge, then follow the setup: where to put your phone and how far to stand.
  • Step into your spot. When the camera sees what it needs, a 3-2-1 countdown starts on its own, so you never have to run back to tap the screen.
  • Move. The stick figure over the camera shows what it sees. A voice counts your reps so you can hear it from across the room.
  • Pay: +40 amber for each finished challenge, +1 shard for beating its goal (once a day each), +1 shard for a new personal best. IRL pays up to 300 amber and 6 shards a day.
  • Private: the camera picture is read right on your phone and never recorded or sent.
  • Camera blocked? On iPhone: Settings → Apps → Safari → Camera → Ask or Allow, then reopen IRL.

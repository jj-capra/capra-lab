HOW IRL WORKS

  • Pick a challenge, then follow the setup: where to put your phone and how far to stand.
  • Step into your spot. When the camera sees what it needs, a 3-2-1 countdown starts on its own, so you never have to run back to tap the screen.
  • Move. The game plays in an animated scene that copies your moves: your lab avatar does what you do, or the ball, football or racket in the front follows your hand. The camera picture sits in the corner with the stick figure it sees. A voice counts your reps so you can hear it from across the room. The workouts (the Full Body Workout, squats, push-ups, wall sit) play in a 3D place you pick in the setup: a wheat field at sunset, a rooftop in the pouring rain, a snowfall under the northern lights or a zen studio (or a tour of all four, a new place every move). Every tenth rep the place answers: birds burst from the wheat, lightning, a shooting star, the gong.
  • The sports: Bowling (swing your arm and roll it; step left or right to line it up), QB Throw (ball up over your head to aim, then throw to the open man), Free Throws (shoot it up from your chest), Tennis Rally (swing as the ball reaches you) and Send It! (flap to push off the pier, then step side to side through the green updrafts, arms out like wings).
  • Full Body Workout: eight moves in a row, about six minutes: Jumping Jacks, Standing Knee Crunches, High Knees, Squats, Cross Crunches, Skater Hops, a Reaction Drill (move the way the arrow says, fast) and Burpees. Each move is on the clock with a 10-second rest that shows how to do the next one. Every rep adds to your total.
  • 2 players: switch it on at the top, stand side by side, and each of you plays your own half of the screen. Whoever’s phone it is gets paid for their side.
  • The camera: tap the camera button for front, back or the 0.5× ultra-wide (when your phone offers it). Turn the phone sideways for a picture that fills a TV.
  • Pay: +40 amber for each finished challenge, +1 shard for beating its goal (once a day each), +1 shard for a new personal best. IRL pays up to 300 amber and 6 shards a day.
  • Phone getting hot or freezing? IRL eases off by itself when a phone struggles. You can also turn on Easy on the phone under the camera button: lighter graphics and a gentler tracker. Plugging the phone in and taking off a thick case helps it stay cool too.
  • Private: the camera picture is read right on your phone and never recorded or sent.
  • Camera blocked? On iPhone: Settings → Apps → Safari → Camera → Ask or Allow, then reopen IRL.

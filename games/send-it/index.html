HOW TO SEND IT

  1. Pick a contraption. Each one flies differently: lift, drag and how easily it stalls.
  2. Tap PUSH! as fast as you can. Your crew runs it down the pier, and more taps mean a faster launch.
  3. Tilt to fly. You ride right behind it: pull the top of the phone toward you to lift the nose (the view tips up into a climb), dip it away to dive. On a computer, drag up and down.
  4. Nose up too far and it stalls and drops. Too flat and drag eats your speed. The meter on the left shows how close you are.
  5. Wind blows in bands. Red pushes you back, green carries you. The radar at the bottom shows them from the side: climb or dive to dodge the red and ride the green.
  6. Farthest out before the splash wins. Every flight pays amber; a new best pays a shard.

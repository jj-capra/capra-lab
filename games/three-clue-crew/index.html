THE ROUNDS

    HOW TO PLAY

    1. Pick your grade level: K–1, 2nd–3rd, middle school or high school.
    2. Every round shows three one-word clues. Figure out what connects all three.
    3. Tap one of the four answers. The host tells you if you got it and why the clues fit.
    4. Ten rounds a game. Today's 10 is the same for everyone at your level, so you can compare scores with friends. Quick 10 is extra practice.
    5. Tap the speaker to have the clues read out loud (it's on for K–1).

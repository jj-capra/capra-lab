MOVE MIND HEIST TO CAPRA LAB

Mind Heist now runs inside Capra Lab (capra-lab.netlify.app/mind-heist/). This folder moves the
players over and switches the old address. Run it once:

  1. Wait until https://capra-lab.netlify.app/mind-heist/ opens Mind Heist.
  2. Double-click "MOVE MIND HEIST.cmd".
     Step 1 copies every record from the old site into Capra Lab (profiles, keys, friends,
     streaks, diamonds, challenges, Vault Jam boards). The old site keeps its copy as a backup.
     Step 2 (after you press a key) makes mind-heist.netlify.app send everyone to Capra Lab.
     Old invite and challenge links keep working, and players stay signed in.

If step 1 stops with a message, fix what it says and run it again: it only copies what is
missing, and the old address is not switched until every record is across.
It uses the Netlify login already on this computer. move-log.txt records what was copied.
